# Marketing delivery plan (internal)

Public summary lives on `/marketing`. Config: `src/lib/pricing.js` → `marketingProcess`, `marketingPlans`, `marketingOneOffs`.

## Phases

1. **Plan** — Goals, audience, monthly calendar.
2. **Create** — Photos, videos, designs.
3. **Publish** — Scheduled posts on client accounts.
4. **Report** — Simple monthly report in plain English.

## What we need from the client

- Instagram / Facebook access or posting rights
- Product / premises / vehicle access for shoots
- Approvals on time
- Brand rules (what they will not say)

## What we do not promise (must stay on the site)

- No guaranteed followers
- No guaranteed sales or bookings from organic posts alone
- No paid ad results unless a separate ads plan is agreed in writing

## Plans (user-facing)

- Monthly: Starter / Growth / Pro
- One-off: Reel, Vehicle showcase video, Event coverage

User-facing product name: **Social Media & Video Marketing** (not “Pulse” on customer-facing pages unless you decide to brand it later).
