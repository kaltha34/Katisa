# Marketing delivery plan (internal)

Public summary lives on `/marketing`. Config: `src/lib/pricing.js`.

## Phases

1. **Plan** — Goals, audience, monthly calendar.
2. **Create** — Photos, videos, designs.
3. **Publish** — Scheduled posts on client accounts.
4. **Report** — Simple monthly report in plain English.

| Lite    | 4          | 0                    | 6,000            |

## What we do not promise

- No guaranteed followers
- No guaranteed sales from organic posts alone
- No paid ad results unless a separate ads plan is agreed in writing

User-facing product name: **Social Media & Video Marketing**.
